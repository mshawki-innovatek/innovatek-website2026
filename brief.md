# Innovatek website brief

## Product

Innovatek SWD is a UAE technology company that designs, integrates, and supports AI-native operational platforms. Its connected portfolio covers charitable giving, facilities and asset operations, visitor management, and multi-channel customer engagement. Flagship products include Donation Hub, Bunyan with Twin AI, Smart VMS, and the Communication Platform.

The promise is: one accountable regional product team builds the operational software, connects it to existing hardware and systems, and supports the rollout after launch. Organizations can begin with one focused solution and connect more modules later.

## Audience

Primary visitors are UAE and wider MENA decision-makers in charitable foundations, government departments, facilities teams, finance, operations, reception, and digital transformation. They need credible regional support, Arabic-ready experiences, integration with their current estate, auditability, and a low-risk path from one urgent workflow to a connected ecosystem.

## Conversion

The single primary action is “Book a working session.” It opens the contact section, where a visitor can enter name, work email, organization, and the operational challenge they want to solve. The success state prepares a complete email in the visitor’s own mail app and states clearly that nothing has been transmitted yet. A direct email fallback is always visible.

## Scope

- A production-grade, bilingual-ready single-page marketing experience using Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, and Lucide React.
- Sections in AIDA order: premium navigation; attention hero; interest through a gapless solution system; desire through a pinned product narrative, outcome-focused platform story, regional delivery process, and FAQ; action through a high-contrast contact section and footer.
- Meaningful interactions: responsive menu, language switch between English and Arabic, expanding solution architecture on desktop and touch, pinned scrolling product chapter, scrubbing text reveal, accessible FAQ accordion, and a contact form with validation and an honest local success state.
- No backend, authentication, CMS, pricing, deployment, or production form delivery in this scope.
- Reference input: `/Users/ahmedtolan/Downloads/Innovatek Landing Page (1).html`.

## SEO foundation

- Semantic landmarks and one clear H1.
- Metadata, canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter images, robots, sitemap, web manifest, and icons.
- JSON-LD for Organization, WebSite, services, and FAQ content.
- Search-focused copy around UAE/MENA operational software, donation management, facilities management/CMMS, visitor management, and AI-powered communication—without keyword stuffing.
- Fast server-rendered content, local or optimized visual assets, descriptive alt text, accessible controls, and no client-only shell for core copy.
- Default site URL is `https://innovatek.ae`, overridable by `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.

## Reference-guided visual system

Preserve the reference’s confident cobalt blue, near-black ink, clean white surfaces, subtle cool borders, regional bilingual cues, rounded interface mockups, verified client proof, and product-led storytelling. Recompose those cues through Premium Web Studio’s selected direction rather than copying the reference page literally.

Art direction: a luminous operations command surface suspended inside a calm Gulf-night editorial field—technical precision against generous cinematic space.

Avoid unsupported customer counts, performance percentages, certification claims, prices, fabricated testimonials, and invented partner logos. The nine client marks recovered from the supplied reference are approved for the immediate post-hero trust panel; all other claims should remain factual, specific, and easy for the owner to revise.

## Constraints and quality bar

- Follow the deterministic Premium Web Studio selections exactly.
- The hero H1 must remain two or three lines at the tested target viewports.
- All bento grids must use dense flow and have no empty cells.
- Use real `@gsap/react` and `ScrollTrigger`, with a reduced-motion fallback.
- No Inter font, decorative hero badges, raw hero stats, banned numbered meta-labels, dead controls, invisible button text, or horizontal overflow.
- Meet keyboard, focus, contrast, touch-target, and responsive-layout expectations at 1440×1000, 1280×800, 768×1024, and 390×844.
